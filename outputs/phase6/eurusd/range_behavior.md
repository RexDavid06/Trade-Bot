# RANGE / CHOPPINESS STUDY

## Dataset

- **Dataset**: `eurusd_m5.csv`
- **Date range**: `2025-06-25 22:45:00` .. `2026-09-10 17:35:00`
- **Candles**: `90,000`
- **Timeframe**: M5 (EURUSD)

## Configuration

- **range_windows**: `[20, 40, 80]`
- **forward_horizons**: `[1, 3, 5, 10, 20, 40, 80]`

## Interpretation

Range-index = `(rolling high-high - rolling low-low) / local ATR`, a measure of how wide recent price is relative to typical bar range. Low range-index = compression/coiling. Buckets are quintiles (Q1 = most compressed, Q5 = widest). We show forward |pips| (expansion) and directional hit rate. Compression -> big expansion but hit ~50% means a volatility trade, not a directional edge.
