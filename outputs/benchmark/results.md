## Key Observations

- Compare dev vs val vs holdout for each strategy.
- A strategy that performs well on dev but poorly on val/holdout is likely overfitted.
- Look for consistent (or at least non-catastrophic) performance across splits.
- All strategies use the same SL/TP (ATR-based) and cost model.
